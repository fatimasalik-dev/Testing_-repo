How It Works

One Platform, Two Ways In

If You Want to Learn

  1. 01

    Create your account and choose courses and tutors.

  2. 02

    Search a skill, compare tutor profiles and ratings.

  3. 03

    Book a date and time. Confirmation arrives by email.

  4. 04

    Meet online or on campus, then rate your session.

If You Want to Teach

  1. 01

    Apply as a skill provider with your results.

  2. 02

    Build a profile: skills, description, availability.

  3. 03

    Accept bookings that fit around your own classes.

  4. 04

    Get paid monthly percentage based on the number of students you recruit.

One account can be both — many of our tutors are still learners in another subject.

Become a Tutor

Turn One Good Grade Into a Monthly Income

Tutoring here is paid work, not volunteering. You set your availability, and your share of every fee is published openly before you apply.

Apply to Teach

Who Can Apply

  • Currently enrolled AIU student
  • CGPA 3.50 or above
  • Grade A or better in the subject you teach
  • At least 3 hours free each week

Applications are reviewed each intake. You will need your transcript and a short CV.
