Become a Tutor

Turn One Good Grade Into a Monthly Income

Tutoring on EduBridge is paid work, not volunteering. You set your own availability, and your share of every session fee is published openly before you apply.

Who Can Apply

We keep the bar simple, so tutoring stays accessible to any AIU student who's genuinely strong in their subject — not just top scorers.

Currently enrolled AIU student

Any programme, any year, as long as you're actively studying here.

CGPA 3.5 or above

This keeps quality consistent for learners booking sessions.

Grade A or better in the subject you teach

You should have demonstrated strength in that specific skill or course.

At least 3 hours free each week

Enough time to commit to regular sessions without affecting your own studies.

What You'll Need

  • Your unofficial transcript or CGPA screenshot
  • A short CV (one page is enough)
  • A short note on what you want to teach and why

How It Works After You Apply

01. Application Reviewed

Applications are reviewed once per intake.

02. Short Interview

A quick call to confirm your subject knowledge and availability.

03. Profile Goes Live

Your tutor profile is published and learners can start booking you.

Ready to Apply?

If you meet the requirements above, fill in the application form,it takes about five minutes.
